What It Means
Designing step-by-step experiments or tests to find answers.
💡 Example: Testing which paper towel brand absorbs the most water.
Wondering "Why?" or "How?" about something you notice in the world.
💡 Example: "Why do plant leaves turn yellow in the fall?"
Using numbers, graphs, and calculations to express scientific facts.
💡 Example: Calculating the average speed of a rolling toy car.
Creating a visual, diagram, or 3D object to show how something works.
💡 Example: Drawing a diagram of the water cycle.
Looking closely at lab results and measurements to spot patterns.
💡 Example: Comparing plant growth charts across three weeks.

Putting Science into Action

Here is how we will use these practices throughout our course:

❓ 1. Drive Our Labs

Every unit starts with real questions. We'll design our own experiments to figure out the answers together.

🎨 2. Visualize Thinking

We won't just memorize definitions; we'll draw and revise physical models as our understanding grows.

📊 3. Analyze Evidence

Instead of taking textbook answers for granted, we'll collect evidence and calculate real data in every lab.

🔄 4. Connect the Dots

Notice how asking a question leads to a model, which requires testing and math? You'll use all 5 skills in almost every unit!
